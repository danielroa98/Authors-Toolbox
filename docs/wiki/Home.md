# Authors Toolbox

**Authors Toolbox** turns [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) into a workspace for writing fiction, poetry and screenplays. It adds a writing panel with statistics and style insights, an English and Spanish spelling and grammar check, screenplay and poetry modes, submission-ready manuscript export, a writing-habits dashboard and a revision mode with drafts and tracked changes.

It runs on **macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad** (Obsidian 1.7.2 or later). Everything except the grammar check works offline, on your device.

![The writing panel next to a story](images/hero.png)

## What it does

| | Feature | In short |
|---|---|---|
| 📊 | [Writing panel](Writing-panel) | Word count, length category, readability, sentence rhythm, a reorderable outline and style insights (repetitions, adverbs, crutch words, names), updated as you type. |
| ✅ | [Spelling and grammar](Spelling-and-grammar) | On-demand LanguageTool check in English or Spanish with underlines, suggestions and a personal dictionary. |
| ✍️ | [Formatting tools](Formatting-tools) | Align single paragraphs, insert images with captions, and type Spanish dialogue dashes (raya) without breaking Markdown. |
| 🎬 | [Screenplays](Screenplays) | Fountain screenplays formatted as you type, with smart Enter, suggestions, a scene board, cast list, coaching notes and `.fountain` export. |
| 🪶 | [Poetry](Poetry) | Verse layout, Spanish *métrica* (sinalefa, ley del acento final), rhyme scheme and form recognition. |
| 📄 | [Manuscript export](Manuscript-export) | Word, PDF, EPUB and plain text in standard manuscript or anonymous contest format, with a live page preview and whole-project export. |
| 📮 | [Copy for Substack](Substack) | A story or poem ready to paste into a Substack post: title and subtitle apart, formatting and verse lines kept. |
| 🔥 | [Writing habits](Writing-habits) | Words per day, goals, streaks, sprints, and where your words go by genre, form and project. |
| 🔁 | [Revision mode](Revision-mode) | Drafts of a note, comparisons word by word and chapter by chapter, restoring text, revision goals, marks in the editor and Word files with tracked changes. |
